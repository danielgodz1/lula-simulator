FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) ❓

🎮 What is Flappy Lula?

Flappy Lula (Lula Simulator) is a 100% free-to-play satirical browser game, featuring the classic 2D Flappy arcade mode and the intense 3D Businessman endless runner.

🕹️ How to play the game?

In Flappy Lula, click/tap the screen or hit Spacebar to fly, dodge pipes, and collect steaks. In 3D Runner, use arrow keys or swipe on mobile to dodge obstacles, trains, and bills.

🎭 Which characters can I play?

You can play with Lula, Janja, Alexandre de Moraes, Nikolas Ferreira, Jair Bolsonaro, Dilma Rousseff, and unlock the legendary Pablo Marçal (3X Quantum Mindset)!

📱 Do I need to download or install an app?

No! The game runs instantly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) on Android, iPhone, Mac, or PC with buttery smooth 60-120 FPS WebGL rendering.

DEVELOPED BY DANIEL DOS SANTOS

Interactive Project · SENAI — Artificial Intelligence & Software Development Program

This game was created by Daniel dos Santos as an interactive capstone project at SENAI, in the AI & Software Engineering course. The game was designed as a humorous and satirical social commentary on Brazilian culture and politics, combining artificial intelligence, real-time 3D graphics rendering, and interactive physics.
